CVOL — CME implied vol index

Annualized % move the options market is pricing over the next 30 days — forward-looking, from real option prices, not a backward-looking statistical estimate. The day change matters more than the level: a sharp jump means the market just repriced risk right now. Where a GARCH comparison is shown, CVOL sitting above it means options are pricing a catalyst GARCH can't see; below it means complacency relative to recent realised behaviour. "History" tracks days accumulated toward the 30 needed before this feeds the vol regime flag automatically (same bar GVZ/VXN already clear via 3yr of free history).
